# Part 1: Test your prompt in AI Canvas (0:10 to 1:30)

**Incident 1: a change breaks the branch.** The night shift starts with a complaint from the branch
office, and you troubleshoot it yourself in AI Canvas. You iterate on a prompt until the answer is right,
add the information only PseudoCo knows, and then turn the prompt into a **general triage method**. That
method has to work for any problem, not just this one, because in Part 2 it becomes your agent's instructions.

| Module | Min | You finish with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [1.1 Orientation](1-1-orientation.md) | 15 | Canvas, Studio and Lab Tools open, your credential added |
| [1.2 Multi-domain diagnosis](1-2-diagnose.md) | 25 | Root cause from Cisco data alone |
| [1.3 Make it yours: Lab Tools](1-3-lab-tools.md) | 25 | Change ID, business impact and severity in the same answer |
| [1.4 Save the triage method](1-4-save.md) | 15 | A pinned Canvas and your general triage prompt |

Before you start, the proctors have injected **one standing fault** that everyone troubleshoots
together. You will not fix it yourself; the proctors clear it at the end of Part 1.

!!! info "Why a branch problem when my table protects a server?"
    Real night shifts don't pick the incident. This one hurts the whole company, so everyone works it
    together while you learn Canvas. Your table's own service gets its turn in Part 2.
