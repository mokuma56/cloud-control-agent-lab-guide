# App design: Night Watch Mission Control

**Build now, without asking questions.** This design is complete: build sections A, B and C in one go.
Section D is an add-on I may ask for later. Where the design is silent, choose the simplest option and
list your choices in the summary.

## Purpose and rules

The morning after: my Studio agent found my attack, raised my card, stopped my own attack and cleared my
card. This single-page app shows what happened and how fast, my group, and the evidence, and lets me
replay the night. It never stops an attack and never changes configuration.

- Use only the integration **Night Watch Console**. Every operation already acts as my own seat, so never
  ask me to type a token, seat ID or IP address, and never show the token. Do show the seat and watch
  source the responses return.
- Always dark, whatever the host's light or dark theme; large numbers readable on a projector. Each panel
  has loading, empty and error states; on error keep the last data and mark it "stale". Load on open,
  refresh every 60 s while visible.

## Data contract (exact responses: use these field names)

Any field may be null or missing: show "-" and keep rendering. Ignore unlisted fields. Lists may be empty.
Times are ISO 8601 UTC; show local time. Only a non-2xx status is an error; on 429 wait 60 s and retry.
Never reject a whole 200 response: check each field on its own and show "-" for any value of an
unexpected type. Types: `group`, metrics, `connections` and the totals are numbers; `is_me`, `open` and
`running` are booleans; `dst_port`, ids and times are strings; everything else is a string or null.

- `get_my_timeline` and `replay_my_attack`: `{seat, group, protects, service, watch_source_ip, card_state,
  attack: {running, started_at, stopped_at, stopped_by}, alert: {open, severity, summary, opened_at},
  cleared_at, metrics_seconds: {time_to_detect, time_to_contain, time_to_resolve},
  events: [{time, event, severity?, summary?, by?}]}`.
  `event` is attack_started, alert_raised, contained, attack_stopped_by_proctor or resolved (skip others;
  attack_started may be missing). Each metric is seconds or null.
- `get_my_group_board`: `{group, seats: [{seat, card_state, severity, agent_summary, updated_at, is_me}]}`.
- `get_my_security_events` (query `minutes`): `{totals: {connections, intrusion_events, file_events},
  connections: [{destination, dst_port, protocol, connections}], since_stop?: {totals: {connections,
  intrusion_events, file_events}, stopped_at}}`. `dst_port` is a string.
- My business service comes with the timeline: `service: {service, criticality, depends_on: {segment,
  apps[]}, impact_if_down}` or null (then say "No business context for this service"). Do not match
  services yourself.
- `get_oncall`: `{oncall: {primary: {name, team}, secondary: {name, team}, policy}}`.
- `get_recent_changes` (query `hours`): `{changes: [{id, system, change, state, time}]}`.

`card_state` is exactly one of: quiet = grey "Quiet"; attack_active = amber "Attack running, not reported
yet"; alert = red "Alert" + severity; stopped_verifying = amber "Verifying"; resolved = green "Resolved".

## A. Core app

1. **Header**: "Night Watch Mission Control", seat, protects, watch_source_ip, a large `card_state` pill,
   "Last updated" and a Refresh button.
2. **Response times**: three tiles from `metrics_seconds` (Time to detect, contain, resolve) as m:ss, or
   "not yet" when null.
3. **Timeline**: `events` oldest first (time, name; for alert_raised the severity and quoted summary), then
   the steps not reached yet, greyed: attack_started, alert_raised, contained, resolved.
4. **My group**: five cards from `get_my_group_board` (seat, `card_state` color, first 80 characters of
   `agent_summary`); outline the card where `is_me` is true.
5. **Replay the night** (header button). Confirm first: "This resets your card and restarts the attack
   from your own watch source only. Your agent acts on its next run, so schedule it for the next 5-minute
   slot." (The slot is advice only.) On confirm, call `replay_my_attack` at once and enter live mode: only
   the timeline polls every 10 s, a "Live" badge and stopwatch show, and at `resolved` show "Your agent
   handled it in m:ss". Leave live mode at resolved, after 30 minutes or on Stop watching.

## B. Evidence my agent saw

`get_my_security_events`: a bar chart of the top 8 `connections[]` from minutes=60 (label
"destination:port", value `connections`), and counters for connections, IPS events and file events from
minutes=5. If `since_stop` is present and all its totals are 0, show a green badge "Quiet since
<stopped_at>". Caption: "Cisco Secure Firewall events: the same data your agent used."

## C. Business impact

My service from the timeline's `service` (criticality, segment, apps, impact if down); on call from
`get_oncall` (primary and secondary as first name + last initial, team, policy); `get_recent_changes`
hours=24 (id, system, change, state), marked "same segment" when `change` mentions my segment.

## D. Add-on (only when I ask): shift handover notes

A shared collection `handover_notes` (group, seat, note up to 500 characters, posted_at). Under My group:
a text area, a Post note button and my group's notes, newest first. A user may delete only their own notes.
