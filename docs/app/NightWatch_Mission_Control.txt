# App design: Night Watch Mission Control

Build a single-page Studio app from this design. Build only section A (the core app) now. Sections B to D
are add-ons I will ask for later, one at a time.

## Purpose

The morning after. My Studio agent watched my service overnight: it found my attack, raised my card on
the room wall, stopped my own attack, checked it was quiet and cleared my card. This app shows what
happened and how fast, shows my group, and lets me replay the night and watch my agent do it again.
The app never stops an attack and never changes configuration. Containment is the agent's job.

## Rules

- Use only the integration **Night Watch Console**, for every section.
- Every operation already acts as my own seat. Never ask for, show or store a token, a seat ID or an IP
  address typed by the user.

## Data contract (exact responses; parse these, do not guess field names)

Parsing rules for every operation: any field may be null or missing, so show "-" and keep rendering.
Ignore fields not listed here. Lists may be empty. Times are ISO 8601 strings in UTC; show local time.
Only a non-2xx status is an error for a block. HTTP 429 means "too many requests": wait 60 s and retry.

- `get_my_timeline` and `replay_my_attack` (same shape):
  `{seat, group, protects, watch_source_ip, card_state,
    attack: {running, started_at, stopped_at, stopped_by},
    alert: {open, severity, summary, opened_at}, cleared_at,
    metrics_seconds: {time_to_detect, time_to_contain, time_to_resolve},
    events: [{time, event, severity?, summary?, by?}], note}`
  `card_state` is exactly one of: quiet, attack_active, alert, stopped_verifying, resolved.
  `event` is one of: attack_started, alert_raised, contained, attack_stopped_by_proctor, resolved; skip
  any other value. attack_started can be missing (show the list anyway). The alert text is `summary`.
  Each metric is a number of seconds or null on its own.
- `get_my_group_board`: `{group, protects, seats: [{seat, color, color_hex, card_state, severity,
  agent_summary, updated_at, is_me}]}` with the same five card_state values.
- `get_my_security_events` (query `minutes`): `{source_ip, window_minutes, totals: {connections,
  intrusion_events, file_events}, connections: [{destination, dst_port, protocol, connections, rules[],
  actions[], applications[]?}], intrusion: [{signature, destination, dst_port, count, classification,
  priority, action}], files: [{file, type, action, disposition, threat, destination, time}], first_seen,
  last_seen, lane_running, since_stop?: {totals: {connections, intrusion_events, file_events},
  last_seen, stopped_at}}`. `dst_port` is a string. `since_stop` is present only after my attack stopped.
- `get_business_services`: `{services: [{service, criticality, business_hours, owner,
  depends_on: {segment, sgt, site, apps[]}, impact_if_down}]}`. My service is the one whose `service`
  appears in my `protects` text.
- `get_oncall`: `{oncall: {primary: {name, team, until}, secondary: {name, team}, escalation[], policy}}`.
- `get_recent_changes` (query `hours`): `{hours, changes: [{id, system, change, requested_by, ticket, time,
  state}]}`.

## A. Core app (build this now)

Operations: `get_my_timeline`, `get_my_group_board`, `replay_my_attack`.

1. **Header**: title "Night Watch Mission Control". From `get_my_timeline`: seat, protects,
   watch_source_ip. A large status pill from `card_state`: quiet grey "Quiet"; attack_active amber
   "Attack running, not reported yet"; alert red "Alert" plus `alert.severity`; stopped_verifying red
   "Stopped, verifying"; resolved green "Resolved". "Last updated" time and a Refresh button.
2. **Response times**: three large tiles from `metrics_seconds`: Time to detect, Time to contain, Time to
   resolve, shown as m:ss, or "not yet" when null.
3. **Timeline**: the `events` list, oldest first, as a vertical list of steps (time, event name, and for
   alert_raised the severity and the agent's summary in quotes). Then show the steps that have not
   happened yet, greyed, in this order: attack_started, alert_raised, contained, resolved.
4. **My group**: from `get_my_group_board`, five small cards (seat, colored by `card_state` as in the
   header, first 80 characters of `agent_summary`). Outline the card where `is_me` is true.
5. **Replay the night** button in the header. Ask for confirmation: "This resets your card and restarts
   the attack from your own watch source only. Your agent acts on its next run, so schedule it for the
   next 5-minute slot." On confirm, call `replay_my_attack`, then enter live mode: refresh
   `get_my_timeline` every 10 seconds, show a "Live" badge and a stopwatch from the replay time, and when
   `card_state` is resolved show "Your agent handled it in m:ss" and leave live mode. Leave live mode
   after 30 minutes or on a Stop watching button.
6. **Refresh**: load on open; outside live mode refresh every 60 seconds while visible.
7. **States**: loading, empty and error state in each block; on error keep the last data and mark it
   "stale".
8. **Look**: dark theme, large readable numbers for a projector.

## B. Add-on: the evidence my agent saw

Add a panel using `get_my_security_events` with minutes=60: a bar chart of the top 8 entries of
`connections[]` (label "destination:port", value `connections`), plus counters for connections, IPS
events and file events from a minutes=5 call. If the result has `since_stop` and all its totals are 0,
show a green badge "Quiet since" `since_stop.stopped_at`. Caption: "Cisco Secure Firewall events: the same
data your agent used."

## C. Add-on: business impact

Add a panel using `get_business_services` (my service: `criticality`, `depends_on.segment` and
`depends_on.apps`, `impact_if_down`), `get_oncall` (primary and secondary: first name and last initial,
team; `policy`) and `get_recent_changes` with hours=24 (`id`, `system`, `change`, `state`; mark "same
segment" when `change` mentions my `depends_on.segment`).

## D. Add-on: shift handover notes (shared app state)

Add a shared collection `handover_notes` (group, seat, note up to 500 characters, posted_at). Under My
group, a text area and a Post note button, and the notes for my group (group from
`get_my_group_board.group`), newest first. A user may delete only their own notes.
