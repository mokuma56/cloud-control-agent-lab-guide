    Build a Network Agent in Cisco Cloud Control That Troubleshoots Anything While You Sleep¶

    You are on call for PseudoCo, and you would like to sleep tonight. In the next four hours you will teach Cisco Cloud Control to do your night shift: find what is broken across every Cisco domain, add the business context only you know, decide how bad it is, wake you only when it matters, and take one safe action you approve.

    The lab follows three steps:

    1. Test a multi-domain troubleshooting prompt in AI Canvas.
    2. Save the combined result so it becomes a known-good example.
    3. Build an agent in Cloud Control Studio that runs it however you choose, with a custom workflow of your own: an integration that brings in non-Cisco information and lights your table's bulb when your agent finds a problem.

    What you leave with

    A working Studio agent, a Canvas prompt you trust, an integration pattern (MCP or REST) you can point at any system your business depends on, and the take-home kit to rebuild it.

    • AI Canvas does the Cisco multi-domain work with its built-in integrations (Meraki, Secure Firewall, Secure Access, Catalyst Center, Intersight, Nexus Dashboard).
    • Lab Tools is the custom integration. It brings in what Cisco cannot know: PseudoCo's change calendar, business services, on-call policy and SaaS status. It also gives your agent its watch scope, the firewall events from your own lane, and two actions: light your bulb, and stop your lane.
    • Your seat token is the key. Everyone uses the same URLs; the token decides which seat, which lane and which light you can see and touch. You can never act on another seat.

    Your table and your color¶

    Six tables of five seats. Your seat ID is T<table>-<letter>, for example T3-C is table 3, seat C. Each table has one bulb; each seat letter has a color.

    Seat A B C D E
    Color red blue purple amber cyan
    • One open issue at a table: the bulb shows that seat's color. Severity sets brightness (minor 40%, major 70%, critical 100% and pulsing).
    • Two or more open issues: the bulb cycles through the open seats' colors, 2 seconds each.
    • The last issue at a table clears: a 3-second green flash, then dark.
    • The projector board shows all 30 seats, time to detect and time to clear.
